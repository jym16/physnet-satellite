# mrgsolve-inspired design notes

This redesign follows the public `mrgsolve.github.io` Quarto structure at the level of layout and tone, not branding. The borrowed design principles are:

- simple Quarto website project with `output-dir: docs`;
- white navbar with gray navigation text;
- restrained Bootstrap/default typography;
- green link accent `#097f5f`;
- minimal CSS rather than a heavy custom front-end;
- landing page as a portal to resources.

The content and images remain specific to the Physical Networks satellite series.
